# A Fresh Start

It's been years since I first started coding. Unfortunately, I never kept my code in a safe place, and I often lost many of the projects I worked so hard on. Despite that, coding has always been one of the most enjoyable things I've ever done. I've always been fascinated by understanding how code works and the logic behind it.

I was in 6th grade (2017) when I first started learning to code. At that time, I had no idea what was actually happening behind the scenes whenever I wrote something in a code editor. I started with **HTML** and **CSS**—I wasn't great at them back then, but they introduced me to the world of web development.

Around the same time, I discovered **Scratch**. I've always loved video games and admired how they were made, so finding out that I could create my own games with Scratch was incredibly exciting. It sparked a curiosity that has stayed with me ever since.

As I progressed through school, I decided to learn **JavaScript**. It quickly became the language I struggled with the most. While my school only taught **Python** and its fundamentals until 12th grade, I found myself trying to understand JavaScript entirely on my own.

I first tried learning JavaScript in 8th grade, but I barely understood anything. I gave it another shot in 9th grade, and although it went slightly better, I still wasn't satisfied. That's when I made a promise to myself:

> **If I'm going to learn something, I'll learn it the right way.**

Even today, whenever I build a website or a web application, I never feel like I'm simply assembling components. What truly excites me is the thinking process—understanding how ideas evolve into designs, how those designs become functional products, and how people eventually use them in their everyday lives.

Growing up without anyone to guide me, I often felt lost. Over the years, I followed people working in web development, app development, cybersecurity, automation, artificial intelligence, and many other fields, hoping to discover what truly interested me.

It wasn't until about three years ago that I finally found my direction.

I realized that what I genuinely love is **designing and engineering products with strong conceptual thinking, solid fundamentals, and a well-structured architecture**. Building things that are both beautiful and functional is what motivates me every day.

Alongside web development, I'm also learning **game development**—something I don't think I'll ever give up. My love for games is what introduced me to programming in the first place, and it's still one of my biggest passions.

---

For a long time, I didn't even know what **Git** was. Looking back, I wish I had discovered it much earlier because countless projects were lost simply due to poor version management. Everything I've learned up to this point has been **self-taught**, and this repository marks a new beginning.

From now on, every project I build will live here—not only to keep my code safe, but also to document my growth as a developer.

Thank you for taking the time to read my story.
